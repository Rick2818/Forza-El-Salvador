# Sales Agent

_Status: PREBUILT_CONFIGURABLE_

**Every opportunity with its next step, without depending on someone remembering.**

Department: Sales

## How it works

- It starts when: A query, a form or a new contact comes in.
- It decides: It separates the buyer from the browser.
- It acts: It prepares the follow-up and leaves it ready for a person to review and send.

## No follow-up leaves without an owner and a date, on every run.

The stages and rules are those of your process, not a closed template.

## Built for teams that answer for the outcome

- Security and data: We declare which data is read and where it is stored.
- Access and controls: Only authorised people can edit rules and lists.
- Oversight: Every send is logged and can be stopped.

## Combine it with more agents

- Customer Service Agent: Every request reaches someone who can resolve it, on the first assignment.
- Marketing Agent: Material that comes from the offer and the context, not from a blank draft.
- Finance Agent: Numbers arrive ordered and with their source, not on a loose sheet.
- Logistics Agent: The state of a shipment is known before the customer asks.
- Chemistry, Pharmacy and Registrations Agent: Files are checked against the applicable rule, not against someone's memory.

## All agents share the same database, permissions and log, so adding another does not mean starting over.
