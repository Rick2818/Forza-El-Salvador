# Clear scope, agreed integrations, supervised implementation

## Security and data

- We declare which data the agent reads, where it is stored and for how long.
- Nothing is sent to a third party without appearing in the proposal.
- Customer data is not used to train models.

## Access and controls

- External integrations require authorisation and certification before they are connected.
- Permissions are limited to the agreed scope, not to the whole system.
- Any access can be revoked without dismantling the work.

## Oversight

- Implementation is supervised: every run can be reviewed afterwards.
- Decisions that affect a person always have human review.
- Every send or change is logged and can be stopped.

## What we do not do

- Published prices are references, not final offers.
- Contracting is subject to an approved quote, taxes and terms.
- External integrations require authorisation and certification.
- There is no 24/7 support.
