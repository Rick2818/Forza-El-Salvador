# Chemistry, Pharmacy and Registrations Agent

_Status: PREBUILT_CONFIGURABLE_

**Files are checked against the applicable rule, not against someone's memory.**

Department: Chemistry, Pharmacy and Registrations

## How it works

- It starts when: A file comes in, or a registration requirement falls due.
- It decides: It compares what was submitted against the current requirement list.
- It acts: It flags what is missing and leaves it for review before submission.

## No file is declared complete without the requirement list verified, on every run.

The rule and the requirement list are supplied by your team.

## Built for teams that answer for the outcome

- Security and data: Files are treated as regulated data and do not leave the agreed environment.
- Access and controls: Only authorised staff access each file.
- Oversight: The final decision on a file is always human.

## Combine it with more agents

- Sales Agent: Every opportunity with its next step, without depending on someone remembering.
- Customer Service Agent: Every request reaches someone who can resolve it, on the first assignment.
- Marketing Agent: Material that comes from the offer and the context, not from a blank draft.
- Finance Agent: Numbers arrive ordered and with their source, not on a loose sheet.
