# Finance Agent

_Status: PREBUILT_CONFIGURABLE_

**Numbers arrive ordered and with their source, not on a loose sheet.**

Department: Finance

## How it works

- It starts when: An invoice, a movement or a period close comes in.
- It decides: It checks that the record matches its source document.
- It acts: It flags the difference and leaves it for review before posting.

## No record is posted without its source document, on every run.

Accounting criteria are set by your team, not by the agent.

## Built for teams that answer for the outcome

- Security and data: Financial data does not leave the agreed environment.
- Access and controls: Only the authorised area accesses the records.
- Oversight: Every flagged difference is logged with its reason.

## Combine it with more agents

- Sales Agent: Every opportunity with its next step, without depending on someone remembering.
- Customer Service Agent: Every request reaches someone who can resolve it, on the first assignment.
- Marketing Agent: Material that comes from the offer and the context, not from a blank draft.
- Logistics Agent: The state of a shipment is known before the customer asks.
- Chemistry, Pharmacy and Registrations Agent: Files are checked against the applicable rule, not against someone's memory.

## All agents share the same database, permissions and log, so adding another does not mean starting over.
