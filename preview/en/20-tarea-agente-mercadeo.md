# Marketing Agent

_Status: PREBUILT_CONFIGURABLE_

**Material that comes from the offer and the context, not from a blank draft.**

Department: Marketing

## How it works

- It starts when: A piece is needed for a specific audience.
- It decides: It picks the argument that fits that audience and drops the one that does not.
- It acts: It produces the draft and leaves it in review before delivery.

## No piece is delivered without human review, on every run.

Tone and limits are set by your team.

## Built for teams that answer for the outcome

- Security and data: Customer data is not used in the material without authorisation.
- Access and controls: Only the authorised team publishes.
- Oversight: Every version is logged and can be reverted.

## Combine it with more agents

- Sales Agent: Every opportunity with its next step, without depending on someone remembering.
- Customer Service Agent: Every request reaches someone who can resolve it, on the first assignment.
- Finance Agent: Numbers arrive ordered and with their source, not on a loose sheet.
- Logistics Agent: The state of a shipment is known before the customer asks.
- Chemistry, Pharmacy and Registrations Agent: Files are checked against the applicable rule, not against someone's memory.

## All agents share the same database, permissions and log, so adding another does not mean starting over.
