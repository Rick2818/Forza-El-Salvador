# From idea to agent

## Steps

1. Tell us what you need: you pick a specialty and the tasks to solve.
2. We agree the scope: we review integrations, limits, budget and timeline.
3. We configure and validate: after approval and contracting, we check permissions and behaviour.

## What you need to have

- An internal owner with authority to approve.
- Authorised access to the tools the process uses.
- Written acceptance criteria before work starts.

## Documentation

### What you need before the call

A description of the current process, who runs it, and what happens when something goes wrong.

### What you get afterwards

The written scope, the acceptance criteria and the start date.

### What we do not do

Connect an integration without certifying it, or promise an outcome that cannot be measured.
