# Customer Service Agent

_Status: PREBUILT_CONFIGURABLE_

**Every request reaches someone who can resolve it, on the first assignment.**

Department: Customer service

## How it works

- It starts when: A request comes in through any enabled channel.
- It decides: It identifies the subject and the real urgency, not the declared one.
- It acts: It answers the repeatable and escalates what needs a person.

## Misclassified requests are returned and feed the rule, on every run.

The categories are those of your operation, not a generic tree.

## Built for teams that answer for the outcome

- Security and data: Requester data does not leave the agreed environment.
- Access and controls: Each queue has a defined owner.
- Oversight: Every automatic reassignment is logged.

## Combine it with more agents

- Sales Agent: Every opportunity with its next step, without depending on someone remembering.
- Marketing Agent: Material that comes from the offer and the context, not from a blank draft.
- Finance Agent: Numbers arrive ordered and with their source, not on a loose sheet.
- Logistics Agent: The state of a shipment is known before the customer asks.
- Chemistry, Pharmacy and Registrations Agent: Files are checked against the applicable rule, not against someone's memory.

## All agents share the same database, permissions and log, so adding another does not mean starting over.
