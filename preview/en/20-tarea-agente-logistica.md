# Logistics Agent

_Status: PREBUILT_CONFIGURABLE_

**The state of a shipment is known before the customer asks.**

Department: Logistics

## How it works

- It starts when: A shipment is created or its state changes.
- It decides: It detects the deviation from the committed date.
- It acts: It notifies the right person and proposes the next action.

## No alert is sent without the source that supports it, on every run.

Committed times are set by your operation.

## Built for teams that answer for the outcome

- Security and data: Recipient data is handled under the declared legal basis.
- Access and controls: Each carrier sees only its shipments.
- Oversight: Every alert is logged and can be audited.

## Combine it with more agents

- Sales Agent: Every opportunity with its next step, without depending on someone remembering.
- Customer Service Agent: Every request reaches someone who can resolve it, on the first assignment.
- Marketing Agent: Material that comes from the offer and the context, not from a blank draft.
- Finance Agent: Numbers arrive ordered and with their source, not on a loose sheet.
- Chemistry, Pharmacy and Registrations Agent: Files are checked against the applicable rule, not against someone's memory.

## All agents share the same database, permissions and log, so adding another does not mean starting over.
