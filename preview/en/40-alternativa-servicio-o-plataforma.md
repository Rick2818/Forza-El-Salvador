# Accompanied service or platform

## Which one fits your team

There is no single answer: it depends on who will build, maintain and answer for the flow.

## Where we differ

- Here someone answers for the outcome, not just for the tool.
- The scope is written before work starts and can be rejected.
- Integrations are certified before they are connected.

## How they compare

| Dimension | Accompanied service | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| Who builds | Us | Your team |
| Who maintains | Us | Your team |
| Who answers for the outcome | Us | Nobody outside your team |
| Scope | Written before work starts | You define it |

## Switching is easy

- We review what you already have working.
- We keep what works and replace only the part that fails.
- Nothing is turned off before the replacement is tested.

## Accompanied service or platform: Frequently asked questions

### Is a platform or a service better?

It depends on whether you have a team to build and maintain. If you do, a platform usually pays off more. If you do not, a service avoids work nobody will do.

### Can I migrate what I already have?

It is reviewed case by case. We do not promise a migration without seeing it first.

### Do you only work with sales?

No. There are agents for customer service, marketing, finance, logistics and registrations.

### Do you work with non-technical teams?

Yes. The requirement is an internal owner, not a programmer.
